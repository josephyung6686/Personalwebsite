## Context

An internship in EY Greater China's Strategy & Transactions practice, which covers financial due diligence and M&A:

- **My part:** go-to-market recommendations for one client, and acquisition screening across two sectors

## Go-to-market for a gaming client

Recommendations built from three kinds of research:

- **Who the customers are:** customer segmentation
- **Who else they could choose:** competitor analysis
- **What people will pay:** pricing and monetization research, with comparables from competing titles

## M&A screening

Two sectors, screened as acquisition targets in Capital IQ:

- **Medtech:** 30+ distributors
- **Hydrogen energy:** 20+ companies
- **How targets were ranked:** screening models ranked them on financial and competitive benchmarks from Capital IQ
- **What came out:** I turned the analysis into investment recommendations
