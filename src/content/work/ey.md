## What I did

EY is a large professional services firm, and its Strategy & Transactions practice works on financial due diligence and M&A. I interned with the Greater China practice in Hong Kong in summer 2026. My work fell into three areas: market-entry analysis for a gaming client, an M&A screen of medtech distributors and a benchmark of hydrogen-energy companies.

## Market entry for a gaming client

For a gaming-industry client, I conducted a market-entry analysis. This kind of work looks at who the customers in a market are, what they can already buy and how those products make money, so a company can decide where to launch and how to position what it sells.

I analyzed customer segments and benchmarked competitor products, pricing, monetization models (the ways a game earns money) and platforms, using comparables from competing titles. That work identified attractive entry markets and unmet customer needs. From there I developed product-positioning and go-to-market recommendations to inform the client's commercial strategy.

## M&A screening of medtech distributors

I developed an M&A target-screening framework for more than 30 European medtech distributors. Screening means narrowing a long list of companies down to the ones worth a closer look.

The framework assessed each distributor on product fit, market access, commercial synergies and geographic expansion potential. Put simply, those ask whether its products suit the buyer, how well it reaches customers, what the two businesses could gain by combining and which new regions it could open up. The screening models also evaluated financial performance, product portfolios and competitive positioning, and ranked targets on financial and competitive benchmarks from Capital IQ, a market data platform.

The result prioritized acquisition candidates for the client to review. I turned the analysis into investment recommendations to inform its target selection.

## Benchmarking hydrogen-energy companies

I also benchmarked more than 20 public and private hydrogen-energy companies, using Capital IQ, IPO filings and comparable-company analysis. IPO filings are the documents a company publishes when it lists on a stock exchange, and comparable-company analysis values a business by comparing it with similar ones.

I synthesized valuation, growth and operating metrics, and looked at revenue drivers and competitive dynamics. The benchmarks supported a five-year listing strategy.
