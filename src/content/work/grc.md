## What GRC is

The Global Research & Consulting Group is a consulting organization with more than 1,000 members that works with nonprofits. Its Columbia chapter, where I started, is student-run and does pro bono work on fundraising strategy, market research, digital presence and program development.

I joined the Columbia chapter in Fall 2025, where I'm Vice President of External Relations and led the Impact Measurement team. Since April 2026 I've also been Global Director of Client Success, on the organization's Global Management Team.

## Measuring impact at Columbia

The Impact Measurement team oversaw client feedback on past projects. For each client interview we sent an outreach note, talked for 15 to 20 minutes and wrote a structured summary of the context, what they implemented, the impact, their ratings and their quotes. We also used a standard survey that asked how a client had used the work, from implemented fully to not used, and which areas had improved.

All of this fed a client impact report. The report used numbers we could verify, and four statistics from an earlier design were dropped because they had no source. One of its four recommendations was to build impact check-ins into future client contracts.

## Guiding client success

As Global Director of Client Success, I guide client success and impact measurement across the organization. I'm the main contact for clients once a project is confirmed, and I stay in touch through delivery, supporting branch and project leaders when client issues come up.

Most of the work is building the processes that make this consistent from branch to branch. I established a centralized client-success function and built its onboarding, its check-in process and its offboarding, along with the way we track client feedback and the impact of each engagement. I also consolidated nonprofit clients in HubSpot, a customer relationship management (CRM) system, so their records sit in one place, and led a team in launching feedback workflows across the organization's consulting projects.

## AI-use guidelines and analysis

I'm drafting organization-wide AI-use guidelines, so that teams across GRC work from one standard on how they use AI.

On the client side, we use standardized evaluations and AI-assisted analytics to identify recurring client needs and service-delivery gaps, which helps us decide which services to fix first.
